Our Team

Kane Streat

Director - Player Contact

Kane is a professional golfer who has played as an Elite Amateur on the U.S. Circuit and as a Professional on the Australasian Tour. He is the former Head Professional of Southern Golf Club on the Sandbelt in Melbourne, has been a PGA Member for 20 years and is well respected in the Australian golf community.

Dave Scanlon

Director - College Contact

Dave has been an elite football (soccer) player and a former police officer of 22 years. 17 years in the Queensland Police Service and 5 years in the Metropolitan Police in London. Dave has established relationships with U.S. College Golf Coaches, negotiated sponsorship deals for current Australian golfers and has a track record of delivering.

Challie McGill

Parent Liaison

Challie is a parent of current U.S. San Diego State student and #1 Australian Amateur, Harry Takis. She has navigated the college pathway herself, ridden the highs and lows of college golf, and supports our athletes’ parents with recent, first-hand U.S. pathway experience.

Harry Takis

Ambassador

Harry Takis is Australia’s number one amateur golfer and knows first-hand what it takes to turn the dream of playing college golf in the United States into reality. Originally from Brisbane, Australia, Harry earned a place at San Diego State University, where he has competed at the highest level of NCAA Division I golf. From the beginning of his college career, Harry has achieved outstanding success, being named Conference Freshman of the Year in his first year and Conference Player of the Year in his sophomore year, before going on to earn All-American Division I honors and represent Team International at the 2026 Palmer Cup, alongside some of the world’s leading amateur golfers.

Harry has experienced every stage of the journey from the initial recruiting process and finding the right university and golf program, to making the move overseas, adapting to college life and competing at an elite level. This first-hand experience gives Harry a unique understanding of what Australian golfers and their families face when considering the U.S. college pathway.

Harry is passionate about helping the next generation of Australian golfers navigate the process, find the right fit and maximise their opportunities to achieve their goals both academically and on the golf course.
